BUILDING PERMISSIONS
Citizens have the provision to apply for building permits online. He / She fills the application form and submits all documents along with an initial payment online. The concerned officer receives the application forms and documents once the applicant pays the initial amount. ACP inspects the site by taking photos using his Mobile phone and updates them in the Report and forwards it to the Addl. CCP for recommendation / rejection of the file. The Addl. CCP reviews the report and adds his remarks and forwards it to the CCP for recommendation / rejection of the file. The CCP reviews the report and adds his remarks and forwards it to the Addl. Commissioner (P&P). The Addl. Commissioner reviews the report and adds his remarks and forwards it to the Commissioner who reviews it, adds his remarks and forwards it to the Building Committee. The Building Committee reviews the report and gives approval or rejects it or suggests revised plans. Upon the file approval, the system arrives at the fee amount. The concerned officer will verify the fee and notify the applicant via SMS and Email. Once the citizen pays the fee, proceedings will be generated by the system and the same will be available online to download and print.

What are building permits and its importance?

Acquiring building permit is perceived to be one of the most significant steps in any kind of a construction project. It ensures that clients protect the outcome and the ‘high’ investment value of the building project. There are endless benefits to getting the building permit. It confirms that the construction building codes are being followed and met during construction and your project will be developed in the right manner, adhering to safety rules and have a long standing.

The building design documentation or architectural plans and permits at the end of the day is a ‘legal’ permission to go ahead with the construction. This go ahead is given after the construction or permit plans ,which includes drawings and specifications, are approved. Permit forms an integral part of the construction process as without it the investment value of the property goes really low. If the homeowners/property owners want to sell their space and the permit was not taken during the construction – they have to pay a heavy some for repairs or not sell it at all. The sign off on permit blueprint means that the structure is safe to occupy All in all, the building permit is significantly important as it lays out the critical rules and regulations that must be followed by everyone involved in the construction or renovation process – homebuilders, contractors, plumbers, and electrician.

The construction or permit plan: the core document

This is a platform to actually show the ‘scope of the construction project’. This is what needs to get approved to even get a go ahead on the project. This drawing needs to include every detail and specifications that are strictly based on the building codes. When the drawing for a floor plan is being done – every room, along with the number of doors, windows, bathroom fixtures, kitchen appliances and closets, and their sizes have to be mentioned. Even the location of smoke and carbon dioxide detectors needs to be shown.
